# Two attack powers and one secondary

From Home, open **Hero Forge / Select Hero**. Choose **Attack Power 1** and **Attack Power 2** from Super Strength, Telekinesis, Fire Blast, Ice, Darkness, Laser Eyes, Lightning and Poison. Choose **Secondary Power / F** from Flight, Speed, Force Field and Teleport. Save & Back stores all three slots in the existing progression save. The HUD shows the same three slots, and synergies still resolve from equipped powers, preferring the attack pair.

In play, **1** and **2** select the attacks and **left mouse** uses the selected attack. **F** activates the secondary: hold it while airborne for Flight; tap it for Speed's dash, Force Field, or Teleport. Teleport blinks 8 m on level, supported ground only when its entire standing capsule can pass a clear corridor. A blocked teleport spends no charge or energy. It uses the existing CharacterController and save/ownership gates.

Existing two-slot saves are repaired on load: an owned Flight, Speed or Force Field formerly in either attack slot moves to the F slot; two owned combat powers fill the attack slots. Ownership, upgrade tiers, XP and suit colors are retained. All four secondaries start owned under the existing roster data. VECTOR's fresh default is Fire Blast + Super Strength with Flight secondary.

Grounded jump speed is now 10 m/s (was 8.5); the one air jump is 8.5 m/s (was 7). At 120 Hz, a standalone ground jump rises 2.0417 m. In the chained double-jump test, the first jump reached 2.0067 m before the second was triggered; the air jump then rose 1.4692 m, for a combined peak of 3.4758 m. Mantle limits remain 0.25–1.8 m high, 0.65 m reach. Flight lift, startup ramp and fuel pressure from the preceding movement checkpoint remain unchanged.
